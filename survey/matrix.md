# matrix — round 1

Folded from the seven surveys on `factory/1-a` (`survey/<family>.md`): subtractive, operator, spectral, drums, sampled, vocal, modular. All seven are surveyed; none is held. Ranked by the vision's round 0 (`instruments.vision.md`, its last and only section): learn the methods, never the lines; what many instruments need, the library gives once; stand beside the open and popular instruments, family by family; a demo the developer can hear.

## Families

Each table is the survey's matrix folded to what the family lacks. A row stands where fewer than half of our instruments hold `yes` and at least a third of the competition's columns do. CPU rows are left out. `yes n/N` counts `yes` cells; names follow when few. `partial n` counts `partial` cells.

## Lacking

Every lacking line of every survey appears here once. Lines that several surveys state alike are merged, and a merged line names every instrument behind it. The ranking runs as follows:

1. The vision's "never the lines" and the library's laws come first, because they stand before any gain.
2. Then the lines that need no engine order, by instruments sharing the lack, most first. Ties go to the line more surveys state, then to the line a demo would make heard. Lines of one instrument keep the commission's family order and the survey's order.
3. Last, the seam lines (`S`): a factory shift cannot build them (391: a seam need is a finding for an engine order). They are ranked among themselves by count.

`n` is the number of instruments. `ground` is the `core/` family the line rides or would found.
